# 0004 — Keep the published Figure 11 locus filter and add a reference-detected set

**Status:** accepted.

## Context

Figure 11 compares per-locus estimates against the 5′RACE signal of SRR4099955. The
published locus filter keeps a locus when `race5 > 1` **or** `value < 10`. The two arms
act on different quantities:

- `race5` is a percentage of total 5′RACE signal, so the cut is unit-free.
- `value` pools TPM (the Salmon-based methods) and RPM (the EM-based methods). The cut of
  10 was calibrated on L1EM output, which is RPM-scaled, so on TPM it removes a very
  different share of loci.

## Evidence

`results/srr_experiment/robustness_filter_definitions.csv` (seven definitions × four
methods, regenerated by `python -m trap.analysis robustness`, seed 3469):

| Definition | n | AlbertSalmon | Salmon | AlbertEM | L1EM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F2 published | 854 / 825 / 280 / 216 | 0.912 | 0.860 | 0.845 | 0.831 |
| F5 common loci with `race5 > 0` | 156 | 0.863 | 0.783 | 0.873 | 0.841 |

The published filter removes 42–44 % of TPM loci and under 1 % of RPM loci, an artefact
of pooling units, not of the threshold. AlbertSalmon scores above Salmon under all seven
definitions, and AlbertEM above L1EM under the five like-for-like ones.

## Decision

Keep the published filter and its values unchanged, and state its provenance and the
per-method locus counts. Add F5 as a second analysis rather than a replacement: a
`race5` of zero is a non-detection, so a set restricted to reference-detected loci
removes the dependence on the estimate-side cut. `notebooks/5.06` declares the two
thresholds once, with units, as `RACE5_MIN_PCT` and `VALUE_MAX_RPM`.

## Consequences

- Cross-method R² under the published filter compares different locus counts and should
  not be read as a ranking across quantifiers; the paired comparison within a
  quantifier family is the claim the data support.
- Removing the dominant locus (chr22:28663283-28669315, 12.2 % of reference signal)
  lowers every method by about 0.27; the paired advantages persist and the cross-quantifier
  ordering does not.

## Reopen if

A filter that is unit-aware on both arms is adopted for the figure itself. It was
computed (F6) but rests on nine TPM loci and is reported as a confirmation only.
